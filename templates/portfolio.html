From Lahore to Global Campaigns – My Story

My journey from a local e-commerce store to managing $2.8M+ ad spend

I didn't start as a "freelancer who runs ads." I started as a digital marketing intern in 2021 managing a local e-commerce store's Facebook page with a ₹15,000 monthly budget. Within 3 months I scaled their revenue by 340% using only Meta Ads. That was my first "aha" moment.

Since then I have personally managed $2.8 million+ in ad spend across Meta (Facebook & Instagram), Google Ads, YouTube, Microsoft Ads, TikTok, and LinkedIn. I have worked with startups that went from 0 to 6-figure monthly revenue and with established brands that wanted to cut their cost-per-acquisition in half.

"Great ads don't just get clicks — they build businesses."

Today I work remotely with clients in the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates, and Pakistan. I have helped a US-based skincare brand hit $87K in sales in 45 days, a Pakistani fashion label grow their Instagram from 8K to 87K engaged followers, and a B2B SaaS company reduce their lead cost by 62%.
